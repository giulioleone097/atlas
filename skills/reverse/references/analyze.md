# Reverse an achieved result into a reusable method

Analytical owner for public reverse and automatic learning. Return to the caller without writing rules or invoking learn/reverse. Reuse a receipt only while its outcome, source revisions, conditions and evidence still match.

1. Bind the actual outcome to its repository/workload, revision and decisive check. Read the supplied diff, benchmark/profile, optimization ledger, review or work record; follow only links needed to resolve attribution. Preserve the baseline, final result, units, noise, guards, setup and observed human/agent/compute/maintenance cost where available. Missing numbers stay unknown. An accepted PR, activity, shorter code or faster test with reduced coverage is not outcome proof. A preparation result proves only its verified property; an unresolved result returns the exact gap.
2. Reconstruct the material sequence: initial bottleneck -> practices/actions and their checkpoints -> intermediate observations -> final outcome. Separate the implementation technique from the development practice that found, tested or integrated it. Include relevant unsuccessful, neutral or unused alternatives from the same evidence; do not sweep unrelated history or tell only the winning story. Preserve what was knowable at each choice. Distinguish practices actually used from improvements proposed by this retrospective: a missing control can justify a future check, never a claim that the check produced the past win.
3. For each consequential practice, state its proposed mechanism, supporting and contrary evidence, prerequisites and plausible competing explanations: workload/setup changes, warm caches, hardware, noise or other simultaneous changes. Distinguish observed contribution, supported inference and untested hypothesis. Before/after alone does not isolate a cause. Keep jointly changed practices as a bundle when attribution cannot be separated; do not add their gains or invent percentage credit. A confirmed failure may establish a limit, not a success claim.
4. Challenge the explanation with the cheapest decisive evidence already available: fixed-workload controls, isolated/reverted changes, repeated measurements or a counterexample. New probes require the existing scope, safety and resource allowance; never rerun expensive work just to fill a template or alter production for attribution. When evidence cannot distinguish explanations, retain the uncertainty and name the next discriminating observation. Analyst agreement or a narrated simulation supplies no causal proof.
5. Derive the smallest supported practice that changes the next relevant action. Compare useful outcome and total effort/cost, preserving correctness, safety and maintainability. Mark steps as required, helpful, unsupported or context-dependent only to the extent evidence permits. A step seen in a successful run is not automatically necessary; a bundle win does not prove each part useful. Fewer lines, words, calls or minutes alone never establishes greater efficiency.
6. State applicability and a case where the practice should fail or be unnecessary. A project-specific gain remains local. Propose broader scope only when the mechanism survives a real transfer case or a decisive falsification check; several copies of the same observation are one source. The learning owner decides promotion and activation. Prefer a conditional default with explicit exceptions over a universal rule.
7. Return outcome/evidence identity, baseline/final KPI and guards/cost, action-to-mechanism-to-result links, attribution limits, alternatives and the candidate rule: trigger/preconditions -> smallest effective action -> proof -> exception/reopen condition. Name the next actual use and its observable check; no invented benefit or reuse count. For material interacting branches use stable nodes and evidence-labelled edges under `<plugin root>/skills/scope/references/composition.md`'s rendering contract. Delegate only useful independent evidence branches within inherited limits; integrate contradictory results, count shared costs once and stop expansion when it cannot change the rule.

Return a compact receipt or `no reusable practice supported` with its evidence gap. No new plan, rule store, research loop or automatic experiment is created here.
