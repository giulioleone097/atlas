argument-hint: "[result or PR] [--drilldown | --kit]"
---

1. Bind the achieved result and available evidence to the request. Read `<plugin root>/core/ATLAS.md` if not loaded; <plugin root> is the parent of skills/. Preserve scope and authority. A future target alone is missing outcome evidence.
2. Run `<this skill>/references/analyze.md`; <this skill> is this directory. Trace every material mechanism/practice and extract patterns by comparing actual instances, conditions and counterexamples. Distinguish recurrence, single-case candidates and validated transfer. Keep detailed support in the existing receipt.
3. Run `<this skill>/references/playbook.md` to derive transferable causal principles from patterns and supported strategies: explain why interventions worked, abstract beyond their means, and challenge the rule in another domain. Deliver identifiable patterns as compact conditional rules, retaining decisive exceptions and uncertain status; a technique list is insufficient.
4. Pass patterns, principles and their supporting receipt to `<plugin root>/skills/ship/references/learn.md` for authorized ownership, integration and reuse. Case implementation is not future adoption; familiar practices still reach the directive. Internal receipts do not expand the public response.

Use concise, navigable causal drilldown as the intermediate human layer when useful. Always finish with self-contained operating rules that name the recovered patterns and express their transferable principles; do not repeat the same rule in separate lists. Preserve decisive qualifiers and candidate status. A minimal-only request skips the human layer, preserving complete actionable principles. --drilldown expands graph/instances/evidence/limits; --kit or an explicit implementations request opens relevant assets and adaptation instructions.

Stop after the transferable principles, requested expansion and authorized learning/read-back, or a precise blocking evidence gap. Preserve the complete supporting practice set without printing a mandatory dossier, kit, table or usage tutorial. No new optimization, commit or release starts here.
